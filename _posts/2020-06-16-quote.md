# {{ page.date | date_to_string }}
I think one of the ways that this has happened a great deal in modernity is that we scapegoat the scapegoaters. We go up one level of abstraction. That sort of always makes it a little more complicated. If we go after the people that were the historical oppressors and victimizers that's often a super powerful way, and it's just slightly too complicated. There was a Bill Clinton formulation of this: "We must unite against those who seek to divide us". On some level it's self-contradictory and it's just a little bit too hard for people to fully disentangle. That's one way I think it still sort of works even though when everyone sees these moves and unerstand them it just doesn't work as well anymore. 

// Peter Thiel\
